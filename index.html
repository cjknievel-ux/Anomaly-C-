Anomaly C

VR Slendytubbies Horror Experience

About the Game

Anomaly C is a VR horror experience inspired by the Slendytubbies universe. Players explore dark environments filled with mysterious anomalies, abandoned locations, and unsettling encounters. Built specifically for VR, the game focuses on immersion, exploration, and psychological horror. As development continues, new areas, creatures, and secrets are being added to expand the world of Anomaly C.

Development Status

The game is currently in development.
